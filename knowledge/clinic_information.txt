5. COMMON MEDICAL CONDITIONS & PATIENT FAQS

- ASTHMA & RESPIRATORY HEALTH:
  - What is Asthma? Asthma is a chronic condition causing inflammation and narrowing of the airways, leading to wheezing, shortness of breath, chest tightness, and coughing.
  - How is Asthma managed at CeylonCare? Treatment includes controller inhalers (corticosteroids) for daily prevention and quick-relief rescue inhalers (salbutamol) for sudden attacks.
  - When to seek Emergency Care for Asthma? Seek immediate emergency care at CeylonCare Emergency (Hotline 1330) if you experience severe shortness of breath, inability to speak full sentences, blue lips or fingernails, or if rescue inhalers do not bring relief within 15 minutes.
  - Pulmonology Clinic Hours: Dr. H. Bandara conducts respiratory & asthma evaluations Tuesdays and Fridays (10:00 AM - 2:00 PM).

- DIABETES & METABOLIC CARE:
  - Diabetes Care: CeylonCare offers Fasting Blood Sugar (FBS), HbA1c testing, and comprehensive diabetic screening packages.
  - Diabetic Clinic Hours: Monday to Friday 9:00 AM - 3:00 PM at all branches.

- DENGUE FEVER & HIGH FEVER GUIDANCE:
  - Dengue Symptoms: High fever, severe headache, eye pain, joint/muscle pain, and rash.
  - Initial Care: Stay well-hydrated with oral rehydration fluids or coconut water. Take Paracetamol for fever. DO NOT take Ibuprofen, Aspirin, or Mefenamic Acid as they increase bleeding risks in Dengue.
  - Warning Signs: Persistent vomiting, abdominal pain, bleeding gums, or extreme lethargy require immediate hospital admission and Full Blood Count (FBC) testing.
