7. CLINICAL SAFETY & TRIAGE DIRECTIVES
- Severe Symptoms (chest pain, acute shortness of breath, loss of consciousness, severe trauma) are immediately routed to Emergency Triage.
- CeylonCare AI Assistants provide informational guidance and appointment coordination; they do not issue formal prescription diagnoses without physician evaluation.
